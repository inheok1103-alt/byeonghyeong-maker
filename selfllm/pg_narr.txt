The moment I stepped onto the stage, my legs began to tremble. Hundreds of faces stared up at me from the dark auditorium, and my carefully prepared speech suddenly vanished from my mind. My mouth went dry, and I could hear my own heartbeat pounding in my ears. I gripped the podium tightly, afraid that everyone could see my hands shaking. Then I noticed my little sister in the front row, smiling and waving at me with both hands. A warm calm spread through my chest. I took a slow breath, and the words came back to me. As I spoke, my voice grew steady and strong, and by the end the audience was applauding warmly. Walking off the stage, I felt as if I were floating on air.
