Dear Mr. Thompson, I am writing as a resident of Green Valley Apartments to bring an urgent matter to your attention. For the past three weeks, the streetlights along the walkway between Building C and the parking lot have not been working. We would greatly appreciate it if you could arrange for the lights to be repaired as soon as possible. A safe and well-lit walkway is essential for everyone in our community. Thank you for your prompt attention to this concern. Sincerely, Laura Bennett
